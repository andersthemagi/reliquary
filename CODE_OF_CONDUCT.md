# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Disagree about code and design as much as you like; don't
attack the person making the argument.

Not welcome here: harassment, personal attacks, sexualized language or
imagery, publishing someone's private information without consent, or
anything else that would get you removed from most professional spaces.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space
where people interact around this project.

## Reporting

Email andres@redmage.cc. Include what happened, where, and links or
screenshots if you have them. We'll look into it and follow up.

## Enforcement

A first violation usually gets a private warning. Repeated or serious
violations get a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from
the project's spaces, at the maintainer's judgment.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
