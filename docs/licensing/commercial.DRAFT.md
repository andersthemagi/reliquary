# Commercial license (DRAFT, not in force)

**DRAFT pending the owner's decision and a legal review. An outline of
terms, not a contract, and not legal advice.** See [README.md](README.md).

Who needs one: anyone making Reliquary's server available to others as a
commercial product or service, which the proposed server license (FSL,
[server-fsl.DRAFT.md](server-fsl.DRAFT.md)) doesn't allow. Running it for
your own organization never needs one.

## Outline

1. **Grant.** A non-exclusive, non-transferable right to run the server and
   make it available to the licensee's own clients, for the term, on
   infrastructure the licensee controls.
2. **Scope.** Named deployments; the number of client vaults hosted is
   reported monthly.
3. **Fees.** Per hosted client vault a month, with a monthly minimum (the
   research suggests $5 and $50); billed monthly or yearly.
4. **Not granted.** The Reliquary name and logo (beyond saying "runs
   Reliquary"), a public self-service competing offering, sublicensing.
5. **Support** (optional, separate): security advisories before public
   disclosure, upgrade help, response times.
6. **Security.** The licensee runs its deployment and is responsible for its
   clients' data; Red Mage has no access to it.
7. **Term and ending.** Yearly, renewing; on ending, the licensee stops
   hosting for others and may keep running it for itself under the public
   license.
8. **Warranty and liability.** As is, liability capped at fees paid in the
   last twelve months (to be written by counsel).

## To decide

Everything above: whether to offer it, the price, the definition of a
client vault, support tiers, the governing law.
